Questions

Straight answers before you request a Snapshot.

Is this another lead-generation package?

No. Top Shelf focuses on what happens after someone reaches out, not on generating more traffic. The goal is to help you keep and convert the inquiries you are already getting.

Do I need to replace my current tools?

Not without a verified reason and your approval. We start by confirming what is already working and build around it where we can.

What does the Opportunity Snapshot include?

A short review of your visibility, response speed, follow-up, and review activity, focused on up to three specific opportunities along with what is already working well.

What happens after the Snapshot?

You will hear directly from Top Shelf with what was found. Nothing is recommended or built before it is confirmed with you.

Is this only for large contractors?

No. It is built for established electricians, plumbers, roofers, and general contractors, most often smaller teams where the owner or office still handles a lot directly.

What powers the technical side of this?

A dedicated delivery platform runs behind the scenes to handle scheduling, messaging, and follow-up. It stays out of your way. You work with Top Shelf, not a piece of software.

Is Agency OS software?

No. It is Top Shelf’s internal playbook for how work gets done. It is not sold or accessed separately.

What information is needed to begin?

Just your business details and a short description of where things currently stand. No passwords or customer data.
